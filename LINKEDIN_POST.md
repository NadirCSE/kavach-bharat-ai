### 💡 What Makes Kavach-Bharat AI Different?

Most AML prototypes rely on generic credit card datasets. We built **Kavach-Bharat AI** ground-up around the real, evolving threat landscape in India:

1️⃣ **The "Digital Arrest" Coercion Detector**: 
With syndicates impersonating CBI and police to coerce senior citizens into liquidating savings, our model flags abnormal RTGS spikes, rapid beneficiary addition, and intimidation patterns within seconds.

2️⃣ **Jan Dhan Mule Smurfing (Section 139A IT Act)**: 
Detects syndicates deliberately structuring micro-transfers at ₹48,900 – ₹49,850 into dormant PMJDY accounts to bypass mandatory PAN reporting.

3️⃣ **Reverse QR & UPI Collect Request Traps**: 
Catches deceptive "Enter UPI PIN to receive cashback" phishing requests by analyzing inverted payment directions and merchant risk scores.

4️⃣ **Graph Network Topology & Circular Hawala Layering**: 
Powered by an in-memory graph traversal engine, it instantly spots closed round-tripping cycles (A ➔ B ➔ C ➔ D ➔ A) and high-in-degree mule aggregator hubs.

5️⃣ **Dual-Language Explainable AI (XAI)**: 
Every flagged transaction includes SHAP-style attribution factor weights, alongside dual compliance summaries in both **English** (for FIU-IND audit logs) and **Hinglish/Hindi** (for tier-1 bank branch managers).

6️⃣ **Automated Statutory Compliance**: 
- 📄 1-Click generation of official **FIU-IND Suspicious Transaction Reports (STR - Form PMLA-12)**.
- ⚡ Instant dispatch of **I4C 1930 "Golden Hour" Inter-Bank Lien Tickets** under CrPC Section 91 to freeze funds before ATM withdrawal.
