### 💡 What Makes Kavach-Bharat AI Different?

Most AML prototypes rely on generic US credit card datasets. We built **Kavach-Bharat AI** specifically for India’s real, evolving threat landscape:

1️⃣ **The "Digital Arrest" Coercion Detector**:
With syndicates impersonating CBI, ED, and Supreme Court officials, our model detects abnormal RTGS spikes, rapid beneficiary addition, and legal intimidation patterns in real time.

2️⃣ **Jan Dhan Mule Smurfing & Sec 139A Structuring**:
Identifies syndicates structuring micro-transfers at ₹48,000 – ₹49,990 into dormant PMJDY accounts to bypass mandatory PAN reporting (Rule 114B IT Act).

3️⃣ **Reverse QR & UPI Collect Traps**:
Catches deceptive "Enter UPI PIN to receive cashback" fraud by analyzing inverted payment directions and merchant risk scores.

4️⃣ **Graph Network Topology & Circular Hawala Layering**:
Powered by an in-memory D3 physics and NetworkX graph engine, it automatically spots closed round-tripping cycles (A ➔ B ➔ C ➔ D ➔ A) and high-in-degree mule aggregator hubs.

5️⃣ **Automated Statutory Lien Freezes (CrPC 91 / I4C 1930)**:
Directly triggers Golden-Hour debit freezes across beneficiary switches and locks fraudulent accounts so money cannot be withdrawn from ATMs.

6️⃣ **Dual-Language Explainable AI (XAI)**:
Every flagged transaction includes SHAP-style attribution breakdown with bilingual compliance narratives in both **English** (for FIU-IND audit logs) and **Hinglish/Hindi** (for tier-1/tier-2 bank branch managers).

7️⃣ **Forensic Analytics & 1-Click CSV Ledger Export**:
Interactive 24-hour circadian threat heatmaps, bimodal risk histograms, payment rail vulnerability breakdowns, and 1-click regulatory CSV export.
